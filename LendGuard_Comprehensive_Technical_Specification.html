1. Executive Summary & System Vision

1.1 Mission & Problem Statement

LendGuard Enterprise v2.0 is an enterprise-grade financial management platform designed to track, audit, and analyze bi-directional peer-to-peer and small-business financial agreements:

  • Money Lent (Receivables / Assets): Capital disbursed to counterparties that must be tracked, aged, reminded, and collected.
  • Money Borrowed (Payables / Liabilities): Capital borrowed from lenders that must be scheduled, amortized, tracked, and repaid on time.

1.2 Inviolable Architectural Principles

  1. Single Source of Authoritative Calculation: No duplicate calculation routines. All metric aggregations derive deterministically from canonical database transaction records.
  2. Bi-Directional Financial Symmetry: Symmetrical depth across ledgers, statistics, dashboards, statements, analytics, and contact exposure for both lending and borrowing.
  3. Multi-Currency Normalization with Raw Audit Integrity: Native currency transactions are preserved unmixed while supporting portfolio-wide conversion into reporting currencies (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, AED, SGD).
  4. Deterministic Auditability & Tamper Evidence: SHA-256 cryptographic snapshot hashing for legally defensible digital statements. Voided transactions are never hard-deleted; they are flagged with mandatory audit justifications.
  5. Zero Mock Policy: Every chart, table, card, and ledger row renders live database state.

3. Domain Data Models & Schema

3.1 Core Entity Definitions

  • UserProfile: Base reporting currency, timezone, phone number, security PIN hash, number masking state.
  • Person: Counterparties (Borrowers, Lenders, or Both) with contact info, relationship tags, archival status, and rolling net balance.
  • Loan: Core agreement recording direction (lent vs. borrowed), principal, currency, interest rate, fixed fee, origination date, due date, status, purpose, and exchange rates.
  • Payment: Atomic transaction ledger entries with amount, currency, reporting amount, FX rate, payment method, void status, and void audit reason.
  • DigitalStatement: Frozen canonical JSON snapshot signed with a 64-character SHA-256 tamper-evident hash seal.

4. Authoritative Financial Calculation Engines

4.1 Balance Engine

Outstanding = max(0.00, (Principal + Interest/Fees) - Total Valid Repayments)
Recovery Rate (%) = min(100.0, (Total Repaid / (Principal + Interest/Fees)) * 100)

5. Privacy & Number Masking Architecture

LendGuard implements a universal privacy mode. When toggled in the top navigation bar, all financial amounts across the entire application (KPIs, tables, graphs, modals, pill strips) are instantly masked as •••••• (preserving currency symbols) and persisted to localStorage.
